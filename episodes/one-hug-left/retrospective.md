# Retrospective: One Hug Left

Episode `one-hug-left`, the studio's reference film. **Backfilled on 2026-11-26.** The shared
engine (`scripts/kit/`) was built alongside this film, replacing per-episode builder
scripts, so its engine history is the whole engine. The measured section only covers runs
after per-stage history existed (the 2026-11-26 compatibility re-run).

## Verdict

Produced without modifying the core engine: no

Engine changes needed: not counted. The engine was written for this film; it is the baseline
that later films are measured against.

## Engine modifications and why

- The whole of `scripts/kit/`: rig, acting, sets, camera, QC, story gate, Blender build, voice,
  sound and captions were written while making this film.
- Not recorded in detail: the film predates the retrospective.

## New reusable assets and behaviours

- Everything in the first version of the library: the four sets (school gate, living room,
  hospital room, hospital corridor), R6 characters with hair, outfits and face atlases,
  walking, sitting, hugs, gestures, looks, lip-sync, number counters, the camera solver, the
  QC rules, the procedural score and foley, and word-timed captions.

## Screenplay revisions

- From the director's review of the preview (see `notes.md`): lens inside a gate post in the
  hook, cropped close-ups and other framing fixes. The full list is in `notes.md`.
