# One Hug Left — production notes

**Logline.** In a town where everyone is born with a number of hugs, a boy born with zero
learns why his "cold" grandfather saved his last one for ten years.

**Brief.** 60 s ± 5, Instagram Reels 1080x1920, narrated by the hero as an adult looking
back, two spoken lines for Grandpa and one for young Kai. Four locations, three leads (Kai,
Mom, Grandpa) and four extras at the school gate. Visual rule: a counter over every head.

## Story (writer / director read-through)

- **Hook (0:00–0:04)**: Lily runs out of the gate into her dad's arms; both counters tick
  down with a chime while the narrator states the rule. The rule is visible before it is explained.
- **Wound**: Kai's counter reads 0. Mom's hug bounces off an invisible wall; the ritual
  that replaces it (she messes up his hair) shows the love the rule denies them.
- **Mystery / misdirect**: Grandpa has had 1 left for ten years and looks at Grandma's
  photo while we hear it, so the audience assumes it was for her. "Not yet, kiddo."
- **Turn**: in hospital, the man who "never hugs" stands up and opens his arms.
- **Reveal**: Kai's 0 becomes 1. "You can't use your last hug. You can only give it away."
  "It was always yours." The ten years now match Kai's age.
- **Choice and button**: Kai spends it on Mom within seconds, completing the hug that was
  blocked at the gate. "Grandpa saved his last hug for ten years. I only saved mine for ten seconds."
- Plants and payoffs: 0 → 1 → 0, blocked hug → completed hug, "ten years" → "ten seconds",
  "Not yet" → "Come here", ruffled hair → held.
