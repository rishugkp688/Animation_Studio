# Retrospective: Three Words

Episode `three-words`, made on 2026-11-26 as the studio's second film, to test whether the
engine makes films other than One Hug Left. **Backfilled on 2026-11-26** from `notes.md` and the
session record, before per-stage history existed; the measured section only covers runs
after that.

## Verdict

Produced without modifying the core engine: no

Engine changes needed: 6. The one that mattered most: parts of the engine only worked for One
Hug Left's cast and sets (the first three items below), so a second film could not have been made from the
screenplay alone.

## Engine modifications and why

- `kit/sound.py`, `kit/captions.py`: the hero chime only played for a character id `kai`, and
  caption colours existed only for Kai, Mom and Grandpa. Both now follow the screenplay's
  `narrator` and cast (optional `caption_color` per cast member). The screenplay could not
  express it because the names were hard-coded.
- `kit/sets.py`, `kit/sound.py`: footstep surface and room reverb were looked up by the four
  existing location names. Each set now declares `surface` and `reverb`.
- `kit/layout.py`: a gesture aimed at a set point (e.g. pointing at a photo) crashed the build;
  targets now resolve to set points as well as actors.
- `kit/layout.py`: the scratch timing used before voices are recorded overestimated length by
  about 20 % (80 s for a 67 s film); it was refitted on the 35 real takes of both films
  (0.26 s + 0.30 s per word, mean error 0.37 s).
- `kit/story.py`: the "at least 3 locations" rule was documented but not enforced.
- Tests: 3 added (`Generality`) so these cannot come back.

## New reusable assets and behaviours

- Shown to work: word counters over heads (the last thing a character said), not only numbers.
- Per-cast `caption_color`; per-set footstep `surface` and `reverb`; gestures that point at
  named set points.
- Staging lessons, written into the docs: hugs need a clear height difference; the person
  being hugged turns to face the other first; walks route around furniture but not around a
  seated person's legs; check the storyboard for backs of heads.

## Screenplay revisions

- n03 was heard as "Mindset, I hate you": respelled with `say: "Mine said... I hate you."`
  (voice gate).
- Six restaging passes for layout QC (35 errors → 0): Leo's legs through the armchair on
  standing up, Leo walking through the bed's foot and between seated knees, the final hug
  interpenetrating, Ava's body blocking Leo's face in sh04.
- From the storyboard: sh16 reveal reframed so "HE MISSES YOU" is not cut off; sh04 and sh20
  no longer shot from behind Ava's hair; sh09 no longer the side of Leo's head.

## Quality-control failures

- Voice gate: one line misheard (fixed by respelling).
- Layout gate: 35 errors on the first full pass (collisions and a blocked face); 0 after six
  passes.
- Build: crash on a gesture aimed at a set point (engine fix above).
- Pacing (not a gate failure): first-second motion 0.74 against 1.0, and 66.9 s against the
  45–65 s target. Both accepted with reasons in the 2026-11-26 picture review.

## Manual interventions

None recorded. Every fix went through the screenplay or the kit.

## Production and rendering time

About 1.5–2 hours from an empty screenplay to a passing preview, by the agent's own estimate
(not measured). Preview render: 0.48 s/frame, about 13 minutes for 1,605 frames. No final
render yet.
