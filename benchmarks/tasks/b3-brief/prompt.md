Write and stage an original short film from this brief, as episode `bench-brief` (create it
with `python3 scripts/studio.py new bench-brief --title "..."`).

Brief: in this town, the last lie you told floats above your head until you tell the truth.
The lead is a kid whose lie has floated there for months. Use only locations already in the
set library. Everything else (who, what the lie is, why, how it ends) is yours.

Take it through the story, voice and layout gates, build the storyboard, look at it and
record the board review. Do not render the film.
