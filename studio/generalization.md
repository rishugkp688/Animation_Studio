# Generalization ledger

One row per film, filled from its `episodes/SLUG/retrospective.md`. The goal: each new,
genuinely different film needs **fewer engine changes** than the one before, until a film
is made by writing only the screenplay. Add the row when the film is delivered.

Next films should be chosen to stress what has not been tried: a different time of day or
weather, more than two leads in one shot, a chase or physical comedy beat, a location not in
the library, no narrator, a non-human-scale prop as the story device.
